Q1. Brute Force approach kya hai?
Har possible buying day i aur uske baad ke selling day j ko check karke maximum profit find karna.

Q2. Time Complexity kya hai?
O(n²) — because two nested loops use kiye hain.

Q3. Buy aur sell same day kyun nahi kar sakte?
Question me different day bola hai, aur sell hamesha buying day ke future me hona chahiye. Isliye:

j = i + 1;

Q4. O(n) me kaise optimize karoge? ⭐
Array ko ek baar traverse karenge aur:

Ab tak ka minimum buying price maintain karenge.
Current price par profit calculate karenge.
Maximum profit update karenge.

Formula:

profit = currentPrice - minPrice

Isse:

Time  = O(n)
Space = O(1)

Q5. Agar profit possible nahi ho to kya return karenge?
0.

Example:

prices = [7, 6, 4, 3, 1]

Koi profitable transaction nahi hai, therefore:
output = 0
